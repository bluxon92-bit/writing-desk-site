The Pad

Your chapter notes, always there.

Every chapter has a Pad. Capture ideas mid-session, work through a problem, track where the scene is going. So your plan and your prose stay connected.

  • Per-chapter scratchpad, always visible beside your manuscript
  • Pulls scene notes from the Plotter automatically
  • Jot ideas without breaking the session
Tabs

Everything open. Nothing lost.

Open character sheets, research documents, and reference files alongside your manuscript and switch between them with a click.

  • Open any reference doc in a tab beside your manuscript
  • Link entire folders from disk and browse without leaving the app
  • Switch between tabs without losing your place in the manuscript
Focus Mode

Just you and the page.

Remove distratctions with a single keystroke for more productive writing sessions.

  • Full-screen focus mode, one click to enter and exit
  • Clean editor background, easy on the eyes
Research Assistant

Answers without the rabbit hole.

A floating panel inside the editor for questions that would otherwise cost you an hour of research. Ask and get back to the sentence you were writing.

  • Ask questions without leaving the editor
  • Fast, concise answers designed
  • Go deeper in one click
Resource Hub

Your story world, always at hand.

A reference library for everything your story contains. Characters, locations, lore, factions, relationships, timeline, glossary, world notes.

  • Eight resource types built specifically for fiction writers
  • Open any resource sheet in a tab beside your manuscript
  • Add images and attachments to resource sheets
  • World consistency checked against your Resource Hub in the AI Editor
