Plot keeps a story moving. Character is what makes a reader care whether it resolves. A novel can survive a shaky structure more easily than it can survive a protagonist nobody believes in, which makes character craft less of a single skill than a collection of them: knowing who someone is, knowing what drives them, knowing how they change, and knowing how to reveal all of that without ever stopping the story to explain it.

This is the map of that whole cluster.

If only one thing gets built before drafting, make it the profile.

Without the profile itself, there's nowhere for that detail to live.
