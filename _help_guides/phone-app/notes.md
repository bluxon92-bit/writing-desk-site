Notes are where new writing starts. They sync both ways, work offline, and there's no limit on how many you keep.

## On your phone

Open the **Notes** tab and tap the add button. Notes support headings, bullets, highlights and comments.

## On your Mac

- Open **Notes** from the bottom of the file name menu in the top bar. A count shows how many are unread.
- Or use the **Notes** button on the home screen, under Recent files.
- Notes are listed down the left, with a red dot on any updated since you last read them.
- Use the three dots at the end of the format bar to **Save as document**, **Link to project**, **Share** or **Delete**.
- Inside a project, Notes shows that project's linked notes first, with a **View all notes** option.

## Turn a note into a document

Choose **Save as document** on your Mac. The note becomes a Markdown document with the full writing environment around it.
