The Writing Desk app for iPhone and iPad keeps your books, documents and notes with you. Read on the train, capture ideas as notes, and edit documents wherever you are. Your edits come back to your Mac for you to review.

## 1. Update Writing Desk on your Mac

Open Writing Desk on your Mac and install the latest version when it offers. The phone features need it.

## 2. Download the app

Get [Writing Desk: Novelist App](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/writing-desk-novelist-app/id6821668661) from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

## 3. Sign in

Use the same account you use on your Mac. New here? Choose **Create account** in the app.

Your Mac app, the phone app and the Formatter all share one account.

## 4. Sync something from your Mac

Until you sync a book or document, the phone shows a prompt to sync from your Mac, or to download the Mac app if you haven't used it yet. Notes work straight away, with no Mac needed.

- [Sync a book to your phone](/help/phone-app/sync-a-book/)
- [Choose which documents sync](/help/phone-app/choose-which-documents-sync/)

## Good to know

- Dark mode lives under **Appearance** in the menu.
- To delete your account, open the menu and choose **Delete account**, below **Sign out**.
- The Android app is on its way.
