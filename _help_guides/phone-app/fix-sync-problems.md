## A phone edit hasn't reached my Mac

- Check the Mac app is open, you're signed in with the same account, and you're online.
- A file you're typing in waits for you to pause. Save or close it and the merge goes ahead. A merge that stalls times out after 60 seconds, and the phone shows why.
- Open the file once on the Mac so Writing Desk records where it lives.

## My book says Read only on the phone

Update Writing Desk on your Mac to the latest version, then switch sync off and on again for the book.

## The phone is empty

Sync a book or document from your Mac, or download the Mac app if you haven't used it yet. Notes work without a Mac.

## I've hit my limit

You see a plain message saying the limit is reached. Remove a document from your phone to free a slot, or swap the book on your phone. Anything already synced keeps working.

## I deleted a document on my phone and still lose a slot

Deleting on the phone hides the document there. Open the file on your Mac, click the cloud icon and choose **Remove from my phone**.

## I moved or renamed a file on my Mac

Writing Desk treats it as a new document. Switch sync on for the file in its new location and remove the old one.

## Two copies of one note

If you edit the same note on both devices at once, Writing Desk keeps one copy marked as a conflicted copy. Keep the version you want and delete the other.

## Word formatting changed

Complex formatting may not survive a phone edit. Choose **Edit a copy**, or restore the backup copy Writing Desk saved when you switched sync on.

## Undo for a merge is gone

Undo lasts for the current session.
