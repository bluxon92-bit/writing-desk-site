The phone app is free to download. How much you can sync depends on your plan.

## What each plan includes

- **Free plan and 30-day trial:** 1 book and 30 documents.
- **Paid plans:** generous fair-use limits for books and documents.
- **Notes:** unlimited on every plan.

## If you downgrade

Anything already synced keeps working, and nothing is removed or paused.

## If you reach your limit

The sync pop-up on your Mac tells you where you stand, for example 1 of 1 books. Remove a document from your phone to free a slot, or swap which book is on your phone.

## Change your plan

Sign in at [writingdeskapp.com](https://www.writingdeskapp.com/) on your computer to manage your plan.
