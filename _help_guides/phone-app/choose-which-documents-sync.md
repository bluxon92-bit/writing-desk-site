Documents sync one file at a time. Nothing syncs automatically.

## Sync a document

1. Open a Markdown, .wddoc or Word file on your Mac.
2. Click the cloud icon beside the file name.
3. Switch sync on. The pop-up shows how many documents you have synced, for example 4 of 30.

## Remove a document from your phone

Open the file on your Mac, click the cloud icon and choose **Remove from my phone**. This also frees the slot.

## Folders

Organise documents into folders on your phone. Folders follow your account across your iPhone and iPad, and don't appear on the Mac.

## Word files

The first time you edit a Word file on your phone, you see a reminder that complex formatting may change. You can edit the file or edit a copy. When you switch sync on for a Word file, Writing Desk saves a backup copy first.

## Your privacy

The path to your file stays on your Mac.

## What the phone can't do yet

- Open a document that lives only on your phone and sync it to your Mac.
- Create a new document. Start with a [note](/help/phone-app/notes/) instead, then use **Save as document** on your Mac.
