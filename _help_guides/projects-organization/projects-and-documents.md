Writing Desk works with three kinds of file. Each has a job, and you can move a piece of writing from one to another as it grows.

## Documents (.md and .docx)

A document is an ordinary markdown or Word file. It's the right choice for shorter writing: essays, blog posts, articles, newsletters and notes.

- Markdown documents are plain text files that open in any other app.
- Word documents open and save as .docx, so they're ready to send to anyone using Word.
- Each document has an outline of its headings, a place to pin references, and non-fiction editorial feedback.

## Projects

A project is the full workspace for long-form writing, such as a novel. It's a folder on your Mac containing your manuscript, drafts, and references and notes.

A project gives you chapters, The Pad, Story Plotter, the Resource Hub, comments, chapter variants, version history and the Formatter.

## Writing Desk documents (.wddoc)

A Writing Desk document is the file format a project's manuscript is saved in. In Finder it shows as a single file, with the Kind "Writing Desk Document". It holds your text along with your chapters, variants and comments.

You can also have a Writing Desk document on its own, outside a project. It keeps comments, chapters and version history, and you can set up a project for it whenever you're ready: look for the **Set up project** badge beside its title.

## Which should I use?

| You're writing | Use |
|---|---|
| An essay, post, article or notes | A document (.md) |
| Something you'll send to people who use Word | A document (.docx) |
| A novel, novella or collection | A project |
| A longer piece that needs comments and version history, without a full project | A Writing Desk document on its own |

## Moving between them

- **Document to project:** use the project control at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Turn a document into a project](/help/projects-organization/turn-a-document-into-a-project/).
- **Document into an existing project:** choose **Add to project…** from the same control. The document is linked into the project and stays where it is.
