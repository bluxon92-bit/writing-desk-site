Documents are Writing Desk's home for shorter writing: essays, blog posts, newsletters, articles and notes. Each one is a plain markdown file (.md), so it opens in any other app and goes wherever you need it.

## Create a document

1. On the home screen, click **Projects**, then open the **Documents** tab.
2. Click **New Document**.

Writing Desk creates an untitled document in your Writing Desk Documents folder and opens it straight away. Start typing: when you close it, Writing Desk offers to name the file from its first line.

You can also press ⌘N, or click **New File** on the home screen, to create a document and choose where to save it yourself.

## Shape it as you write

Use headings to structure your piece. Choose them from the **Normal** menu in the toolbar, press ⌥⌘1 to ⌥⌘3, or type `#`, `##` or `###` and a space at the start of a line.

Every heading appears in the **Outline** at the top of the sidebar. Click one to jump to it.

## Keep your sources to hand

Pin the files, folders and links you're drawing on in **References**, at the bottom of the sidebar. Click **+**, choose what to pin, and it stays one click away whenever you open this document.

## Keep your documents organised

The **Documents** tab is where all your documents live.

- **Favourites** lists the documents you've opened recently or marked as favourites, so the ones you're working on are always at the top. Click **Add favourite** to add one.
- **Documents** shows your Writing Desk Documents folder. Click **Add folder** to create a new folder, or **Add file** to create a new document, and drag files and folders to reorganise them.

## Get feedback before you publish

When your draft is ready, open **Analysis** and choose **AI Editor** for feedback built for non-fiction. See [Get editorial feedback on non-fiction](/help/editorial-ai/non-fiction-feedback/).

Writing Desk saves your document automatically as you go, so there's nothing to remember.
