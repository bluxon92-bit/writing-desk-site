Writing Desk has two places you'll spend your time: the home screen, where you choose what to work on, and the editor, where you write. Everything sits on soft, floating panels, so your words stay at the centre.

## The home screen

When you open Writing Desk you'll see three buttons, with your recently opened files listed underneath.

- **Projects** opens a window with two tabs. **Projects** holds your long-form work, such as a novel. **Documents** holds your shorter writing, such as essays, blog posts and notes.
- **New File** creates a new markdown document and asks where to save it.
- **Open File** opens any Writing Desk document (.wddoc), Word file (.docx) or markdown file (.md) on your Mac.

Click any file under **Recent** to pick up where you left off.

## The editor

Your writing sits in the middle of the screen, with the toolbar floating above it.

### Along the top

- **The file title**, top left, shows what you're working on. Click the arrow beside it to switch between open files. The badge next to it shows the file type, such as `.MD`.
- **The toolbar** holds the paragraph style menu (Normal, Heading 1 and so on), formatting, alignment and lists. The **⋯** button opens more formatting options.
- **The menu (☰)**, at the left of the toolbar, holds export, Send to Formatter and your other file options.
- **Comments** (the speech-bubble icon) opens the comments sidebar.
- **Analysis** opens the proofreading and editorial tools.
- **The Writing Tracker** sits at the top right, alongside the autosave status.

### The left sidebar

The sidebar changes depending on what you're working on.

- **In a project**, it holds your chapters, The Pad for chapter notes and Story Plotter, with the Resource Hub at the bottom.
- **In a document**, it shows an **Outline** of your headings at the top and **References** at the bottom, where you can pin the files, folders and links you're drawing on. The control below that shows whether the document belongs to a project, and lets you add it to one.

Hide or show the sidebar with ⌃⌘S, or the sidebar button at its top right.

## Writing without distractions

Press ⇧⌘F to enter Focus Mode, which hides everything except your writing. Press ⇧⌘F again, or Esc, to come back.

## Shortcuts and help as you go

Press ⌘/ at any time to see every keyboard shortcut. For formatting as you type, such as `##` for a chapter heading, open **Help → Markdown Reference** from the menu bar.
