Writing Desk is the desktop app for plotting and drafting your novel on a Mac. Formatter is where you review, typeset, and export, and it also works in the browser for reading your manuscript or making light edits when you're on the go.

Where your novel lives.

Writing Desk is the full writing environment in one place. Plot your story with the Story Plotter, draft distraction-free in the editor, keep research and character notes alongside your manuscript, and track your progress as you go.
