FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Writing Desk?

Writing Desk is a book writing software for Mac, built for novelists. It brings plotting, drafting, editing, tracking, and formatting into one place, so you're not juggling five different tools to finish a book.

Who is Writing Desk for?

Writing Desk is built for creative writers and novelists. Whether you're outlining your first book or finishing your tenth, it's built around how long-form fiction actually gets written, not generic word processing. If you've outgrown scattered notes and other writing apps that weren't built for novels, this is for you.

How is Writing Desk different from other book writing software?

Writing Desk is book writing software that brings every stage of writing a novel into one place.

Start with the story planning tools, build out character sheets and world-building notes, add images, and grow your reference folder as you go.

When you're ready to write, move into a distraction-free editor with everything close at hand.

When you're ready to edit, use the AI Editor alongside Prose Analysis tools like passive voice detection and showing vs. telling to polish your writing. The AI tools are editorial only, they help you see your work more clearly, but they never write your prose for you.

And when you're ready to publish, export in a format ready for agent submission, or as an EPUB or print-ready PDF, all from one easy-to-use, end-to-end novel writing software.

Does Writing Desk use AI to write my book for me?

No. Writing Desk's AI tools give feedback only. Writing Desk will never write prose on your behalf. Developmental notes, line edits, consistency checks, a second pair of eyes. The words on the page stay yours.

Is my manuscript private?

Yes. Writing Desk is a private, local writing app. Your manuscript stays on your machine until you're readyfor it to be shared and is never used to train any AI model.

Can I import my manuscript from Scrivener or Word?

Getting started with Writing Desk is simple. Export your manuscript from Scrivener, Word, or any other writing app as a .docx file, then simply open it directly in Writing Desk. No complicated import process, no long setup.

Can I use Writing Desk offline?

Yes, for the core of your writing. Drafting, editing, proofreading, and tracking all work without an internet connection. AI-powered tools need a connection to run.

Can I use Writing Desk on PC?

Not yet. Writing Desk is Mac-only for now, with Windows on the roadmap.
