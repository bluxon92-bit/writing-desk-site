Writing Desk is built on a simple principle: your manuscript is yours. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, what we don't, and how everything is handled. We've tried to write it in plain English.

1. Overview

Writing Desk is a native macOS application, with a companion app for iPhone, iPad and Android tablets and phones. On the Mac, your manuscript lives as a file on your own machine. Unless you choose to send something to Formatter or sync it to your phone (both explained below), your writing is not transmitted to our servers. We never use it to train AI models, and neither does anyone else.

The data we do collect is limited to what's necessary to operate the service: account information, the notes, books and documents you choose to sync, a record of which of your devices are signed in, anonymised usage analytics in the desktop app and Formatter, and brief operational logs from AI API calls.

2. Your manuscript

Writing Desk stores your manuscript as a .docx file and a companion .wdjson sidecar file on your Mac. These files stay on your machine unless you choose to send a copy to Formatter (section 5) or turn on syncing to your phone (section 3). They are not otherwise uploaded to, synced with, or stored on Writing Desk's servers.

If you choose to use cloud sync (iCloud, Dropbox, or similar), your files are handled according to that service's own privacy policy. Writing Desk does not control or have access to files stored in third-party cloud services.

We make no claim of ownership over your manuscript or any content you create using Writing Desk.

3. Phone & tablet app

The Writing Desk app for iPhone, iPad and Android uses the same account as the Mac app. It keeps your notes and the books and documents you sync in a database inside the app on your device, so it works offline.

What is sent to our servers, and when:

  • Account: you can create an account in the app with an email address and password, or sign in with the one you use on the Mac
  • Notes: notes you write on your Mac or phone are stored in your account so they appear on both
  • Books: only when you switch sync on for a book on your Mac. A copy of the chapters is stored in your Formatter library (section 5) and delivered to your phone. Turning sync off stops further sending
  • Documents: only the Markdown and standalone Writing Desk documents you choose to sync from your Mac. Word (.docx) files are never synced, and the location of a file on your Mac is never sent to us
  • Edits made on your phone: sent back to your account so your Mac can offer them to you as changes to accept or reject
  • Device record: each app records that it is signed in to your account, with a random device identifier, the type of device (phone or computer), the platform (iOS, Android or macOS), the app version and when it was last active

We do not read your notes, books or documents, we do not share them, and they are never used to train AI models. You can remove a synced book or document from your phone at any time, and deleting your account removes everything stored for it (section 6).

The phone app does not currently include usage analytics, advertising or third-party tracking, and it does not send your writing to any AI service.

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup) may include the app's local data. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies, not by us.

4. AI features

Writing Desk's AI editorial features — including the Research Assistant, AI Editor, and Full Manuscript Review — are powered by the Anthropic API.

When you use one of these features, the relevant content (a chapter, a research query, or your full manuscript) is sent to Anthropic's API over an encrypted connection. A response is returned and displayed in the app. This content is not retained by Writing Desk.

Anthropic's API data policy applies to content processed through the API:

  • API inputs and outputs are stored in operational logs for up to 7 days, then automatically deleted
  • API data is never used to train Anthropic's AI models
  • This is a flat policy with no exceptions for API customers

You can read Anthropic's full data policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Writing Desk will never generate prose on your behalf. AI features exist to assist your thinking and editorial process. Everything written in your manuscript is your own work.

5. Print-ready export & Formatter

Generating a professionally typeset print-ready PDF requires server-side processing — this is the nature of the task and cannot be done entirely on-device. This work happens in Writing Desk Formatter, our companion web app.

When you send a manuscript to Formatter for typesetting — using "Typeset & Print" from the desktop app, by turning on phone sync for a book, or by uploading directly:

  • A copy of your manuscript is transmitted to our servers over an encrypted connection and stored so Formatter can display it, let you adjust layout, and generate exports
  • We do not read, review, analyse, or otherwise use your manuscript content for any purpose beyond providing the typesetting and export service you've asked for
  • Your manuscript is never shared with anyone, and never used to train AI models, by us or anyone else
  • You can delete your manuscript from Formatter at any time from your library — deletion is immediate and permanent

Separately, once a PDF is generated:

  • You are asked whether you would like us to retain the formatted file for future use, or delete it immediately
  • If you choose deletion, the file is removed from our servers within 24 hours of delivery
  • If you choose retention, your formatted file is stored securely in your account and accessible from your exports library

The choice on the formatted file is always yours, and the default is deletion. The source manuscript itself stays in your Formatter library, under your control, until you choose to remove it — this just means you can generate further exports without re-uploading.

6. Account data

A Writing Desk account is required for premium and Pro subscription features, for Formatter, and to use the phone and tablet app. You can create one in the Mac app, in the phone app, or on the website. When you create an account we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A hashed password (we never store your password in plain text)
  • Your subscription plan and billing status (payment processing is handled by Stripe — we do not store card details)
  • The date your account was created

All payment processing is handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor — the highest level of certification in the payments industry. Your card details are sent directly to Stripe and never pass through or touch our servers.

We use your email address to send account-related communications (receipts, subscription updates, password resets). We do not send marketing email without your explicit opt-in.

You can delete your account at any time from within account settings in the Mac app, or from the menu in the phone app (Menu, then Delete account). Deletion removes your account and everything stored for it, including your notes, synced books and documents, Formatter library, exports and device records, and cancels any active subscription. It is carried out as soon as you confirm, and in any case within 30 days. If you cannot get into the app, email help@writingdeskapp.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. Deleting the app from your device on its own does not delete your account.

7. In-app usage analytics

The Writing Desk desktop app and Formatter collect anonymised usage analytics — standard telemetry used to operate and improve the service. This is how we learn which features get used, where people drop off during onboarding, and how the app is used day to day, so we can make it better.

This never includes your manuscript, chapter text, or any content you write. It's limited to feature-level events — for example, "chapter review run" or "EPUB exported" — plus an anonymised identifier used to connect your activity within your own account across the desktop app and Formatter.

This is handled through PostHog, hosted in the EU. Because this data is anonymised and limited to operating the service, it falls within normal use needed to run the product and doesn't require a separate consent step under UK GDPR — it's disclosed here instead. If you'd like more detail on exactly what's collected, email us and we're happy to walk through it.

8. Website analytics

The Writing Desk website uses Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymised usage data — pages visited, time on page, device type, and approximate location (country/region level). This data helps us understand how the site is used and improve it.

No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics. You can opt out of analytics tracking via your browser's cookie settings or by using a browser extension such as uBlock Origin.

We use a cookie consent notice on first visit. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

9. Email and mailing list

If you sign up for Writing Desk updates or download a free resource from this site, your email address will be added to our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send. We will never sell or share your email address with third parties.

10. Third-party services

Writing Desk uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Anthropic API — powers AI editorial features. Data policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Supabase — hosts our database, sign-in and file storage, including your account, notes and synced writing. Our Supabase project is hosted in the EU (Ireland). Policy: supabase.com/privacy
  • Stripe — handles subscription payments, PCI-DSS Level 1 certified. We do not store payment card details. Policy: stripe.com/gb/privacy
  • PostHog — anonymised in-app usage analytics for the desktop app and Formatter, EU-hosted. Policy: posthog.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymised website analytics. Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • iCloud / Dropbox — optional cloud sync, used only if you configure it. Data is governed by your agreement with those services.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — distribute the phone app. They receive download information under their own policies; we do not receive your payment details from them.

11. Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email help@writingdeskapp.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

12. Children

Writing Desk is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
