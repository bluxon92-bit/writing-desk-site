4. AI features

Writing Desk's AI editorial features — including the Research Assistant, AI Editor, and Full Manuscript Review — are powered by the Anthropic API. When you use these features, relevant content from your manuscript is sent to Anthropic's API for processing and a response is returned. This content is not retained by Anthropic beyond a 7-day operational log window and is never used to train AI models. Writing Desk does not store or retain manuscript content sent through these features.

Writing Desk will never generate prose on your behalf. AI features are designed to assist your thinking and editorial process — not to write your manuscript. Everything on the page is your own work.
